    Analysis, Agent Architecture, Platform Optimization
  - 6 Weekly Business Programs: Emotional Eating, Content Marketing, Competitive Intel,
    Revenue Strategy, Companion Messages, Legal Framework

  How it works:
  1. Research schedules trigger programs (nightly at 2 AM CT, weekly Sundays at midnight CT)
  2. Each session runs 5-15 experiments using the keep/discard loop
  3. Findings scoring 6+ auto-inject into knowledge base with vector embeddings
  4. High-score findings (8+) generate code proposals
  5. Cross-persona routing sends findings to the right specialist
  6. Self-healing backpressure handles DB load automatically
